Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.
Last updated: 14 November 2026

01. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the ILSRP Discord Bot ("the Bot") collects, uses, stores and protects information when used in Discord servers.

By using the Bot, you acknowledge this policy.

02. Information We Collect

The Bot may collect and store:

  • Discord user IDs and server IDs.
  • Discord usernames and server configuration information.
  • Role IDs and channel IDs used for permissions and logging.
  • Ticket information and transcripts.
  • Moderation records, including warnings and staff actions.
  • Roblox usernames and Roblox user IDs when verification is used.
  • ER:LC private server API keys supplied by authorized administrators.
  • ER:LC server information and player data when requested.

03. How We Use Information

Information is used to provide Bot functionality, manage tickets and moderation, verify Roblox accounts, connect to ER:LC servers, execute authorized commands, maintain security and troubleshoot technical problems.

04. Storage and Security

Configuration and operational information may be stored in the Bot's database.

ER:LC API keys are stored as part of the relevant Discord server's configuration and are not intentionally displayed in public responses.

Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

05. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by Discord, Roblox and the ER:LC API when necessary to provide requested functionality.

Server administrators may access information generated by the Bot within their own Discord server, subject to their permissions.

06. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Bot's services and maintain operational records.

Discord server administrators may request removal of their server's stored configuration and associated data by contacting the Bot's developer.

Some records may remain in backups or logs for a limited period.

07. Third-Party Services

The Bot interacts with Discord, Roblox and the Emergency Response: Liberty County (ER:LC) API. These services have their own privacy policies and terms.

08. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Bot's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL.

09. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, contact the Bot's developer ( Ilyesilyes1415 / Rafale Dude) through the official ILSRP Discord support server.
